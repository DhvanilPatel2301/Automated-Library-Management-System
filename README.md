> **The Catalyst for Change:** Our department was relying on offline, manual book registers to track library assets. This led to severe bottlenecks, lost time during checkouts, and major administrative headaches when calculating inventory at the end of the year. This system completely digitized the workflow.

## 🚀 The Department Problem (Before)

Previously, both students and management faced significant friction due to the manual paper-based logging system:

<div align="center">

| 🧑‍🎓 Friction for Students | 📋 Administrative Headaches for Management |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Long Queues:** Students had to wait in line for the manual register to free up before issuing a book.<br><br>**Slow Returns:** Returning a book meant manually flipping through pages to find the exact past date and name record. | **Manual Calculations:** End-of-year calculations (e.g., total books issued) had to be counted by hand.<br><br>**Blind Inventory:** Management couldn't easily track which books were the most demanded, leading to frequent book shortages and poor procurement decisions. |

</div>

---

## 💡 The Automated Solution (After)

To eliminate these bottlenecks, I engineered a **fully online, cloud-synced system** utilizing Google Forms and Google Sheets, completely bypassing the need for offline registers.

### 🔑 Key Features & Workflow

1. **Concurrent Form Submissions (QR Scans):**
   Students simply scan a **QR Code** at the desk for "Book Issue" or "Book Return." Multiple students can fill out the form simultaneously on their own phones, eliminating all queues.

2. **Live Data Fetching:**
   Every submission is instantly synced to a centralized Google Sheet, giving management a live dashboard of current inventory, issued books, and outstanding returns.

3. **Instant Proof of Issue:**
   The system triggers an automated email immediately upon form submission, providing the student with a digital receipt (including the date and book details).

4. **14-Day Automated Reminders:**
   If a book is not returned within the designated period, the system automatically dispatches a reminder email to the student exactly 14 days after the issue date, drastically improving return times.

---

## 📈 Problems Solved

By digitizing this workflow, the department achieved:
- **Instant Data Retrieval:** Management can now query data instantly using Excel/Google Sheets filters rather than flipping through manual registers.
- **Data-Driven Procurement:** The system automatically identifies the "Most Issued Books," allowing management to order more copies of high-demand titles and avoid future shortages.
- **Improved Compliance:** Automated email reminders successfully engineered a shift in student behavior, resulting in vastly improved on-time return rates.
